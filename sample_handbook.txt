1. WORKING HOURS

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Employees
may request a flexible schedule by speaking with their manager. Core hours,
during which everyone is expected to be available for meetings and
collaboration, are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Outside of core hours, employees are
free to arrange their start and end times as long as they complete a full
eight-hour day and meet their commitments. Overtime must be approved in advance
by a manager and is compensated at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate for
eligible employees.


2. PAID TIME OFF AND VACATION

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per year, accruing at a rate
of roughly 1.67 days per month worked. In addition to vacation, the company
observes 10 public holidays each year. Vacation requests should be submitted at
least two weeks in advance through the company portal so that managers can plan
around team coverage. Unused vacation of up to 5 days may be carried over into
the following calendar year; any balance above 5 days is forfeited at year end.
New employees begin accruing vacation from their first day but may not take
vacation during their first 90 days of employment without manager approval.


3. SICK LEAVE

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Unlike vacation, sick leave does
not roll over into the next year and is not paid out when an employee leaves the
company. A doctor's note is required for any sick absence longer than three
consecutive days. Employees who are unwell are strongly encouraged to stay home
and rest rather than risk spreading illness to colleagues. Sick leave may also
be used to care for an immediate family member who is ill.


4. REMOTE WORK AND HYBRID SCHEDULES

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require director approval and are reviewed every six months. The company
provides a one-time stipend of 500 dollars for home office equipment such as a
chair, desk, or monitor. Employees working remotely must remain reachable during
core hours and must ensure they have a secure and private internet connection
when handling confidential information. Remote work is a privilege and may be
revoked if performance or communication suffers.


5. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT

Work-related expenses are reimbursed within 30 days of submission. Receipts are
required for any expense over 25 dollars. Travel must be booked through the
company travel portal in order to qualify for reimbursement. Meals during
business travel are reimbursed up to a daily limit of 60 dollars. Personal
expenses, fines, and alcohol are never reimbursable. Questions about whether a
specific expense qualifies should be directed to the finance team before the
expense is incurred.


6. REFUND AND RETURN POLICY (CUSTOMERS)

Customers may return any widget within 60 days of purchase for a full refund,
provided the widget is undamaged and returned in its original packaging. Refunds
are processed to the original payment method within 14 business days of the
return being received. Custom-engraved widgets are made to order and are
therefore non-refundable. Shipping charges are not refunded except in cases
where the widget arrived damaged or defective. To start a return, customers
should use the returns portal and print the prepaid shipping label provided.


7. PARENTAL LEAVE

The company offers 12 weeks of paid parental leave to all full-time employees,
regardless of gender, following the birth or adoption of a child. Parental leave
must be taken within the first 12 months and may be taken all at once or split
into two separate blocks. Employees should notify their manager and Human
Resources at least 30 days before the expected start of leave when possible.


8. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Each employee is allocated an annual training budget of 1,000 dollars for
courses, books, conferences, and certifications relevant to their role. Requests
are approved by the employee's manager. The company also runs internal lunch-
and-learn sessions every month and encourages employees to share knowledge with
their teams.


9. CODE OF CONDUCT

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with
respect and professionalism. Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind
is grounds for disciplinary action up to and including termination. Concerns may
be reported confidentially to the Human Resources department, and the company
prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.
